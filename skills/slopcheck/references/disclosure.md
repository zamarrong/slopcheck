# Disclosure norms

slopcheck exists so that text a human signs reads like a human wrote it. That is a
question of craft. Whether the human owed someone a disclosure is a separate question,
and cleaning up prose never answers it.

None of the rules below govern internal company documents, which is where most
unreviewed generated text actually circulates. They are the closest thing to a settled
norm, and they are worth knowing before arguing about the topic in public.

## Academic and scientific publishing

**ICMJE** (International Committee of Medical Journal Editors) and **COPE** (Committee
on Publication Ethics) converge on three points:

1. An AI system cannot be an author. Authorship requires accountability, and a model
   cannot be accountable.
2. Authors must disclose use: which tool, which version, which functions, and which
   sections of the manuscript.
3. The human author remains responsible for everything in the text, including errors,
   fabricated citations and undetected plagiarism.

Several 2025 guidelines additionally prohibit the use of generative AI in peer review,
on confidentiality grounds.

The recommended format is a statement at the end of the work, commonly titled
"Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process".

## European Union

The **AI Act** (Regulation 2024/1689), article 50, sets transparency obligations for
generated content, with a Code of Practice developed by the Commission. It binds
deployers and providers, not individual writers, and it establishes the direction
of travel: generated content carries a disclosure duty proportional to its reach.

## A practical position

Ask what the reader would want to know, and what they would feel misled by afterwards.

Nobody expects a disclosure for a spell-check or a rephrased sentence. A reader who
believes they are reading your reasoning, when the reasoning was generated and passed
along unreviewed, has been misled regardless of how the prose reads.

The test that survives contact with reality: **can you defend every claim in the
document without the file open?** If not, the problem is not disclosure. The problem is
that nobody has done the work yet.
