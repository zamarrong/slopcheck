# Sources

## Pattern catalogues

- **Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing
  The most complete public catalogue: roughly 15,000 words maintained by WikiProject AI
  Cleanup from thousands of flagged submissions. Documents negative parallelisms,
  inflated vocabulary, trailing participles, vague attribution, formatting habits,
  "Challenges and Future Directions" style conclusions, and the measured drop in copula
  frequency. Explicitly descriptive rather than prescriptive: observations, not rules.

- **TechCrunch on that guide** (Nov 2025) —
  https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/20/the-best-guide-to-spotting-ai-writing-comes-from-wikipedia/

## A note on detectors

This project cites no AI-detection literature and implements none of it. Statistical
detectors have well-documented false-positive rates, and those errors fall hardest on
non-native writers. slopcheck counts constructions and shows them to the author. It
makes no claim about origin, and it should never be cited as evidence about a person.
